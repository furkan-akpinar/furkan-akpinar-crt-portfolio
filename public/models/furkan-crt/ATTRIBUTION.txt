Furkan CRT Computer

Created specifically for Furkan Akpinar's portfolio.
The cabinet, curved display, keyboard and detail geometry were authored
procedurally. The small surface-detail and lettering textures were generated
for this model. No third-party mesh or texture is embedded in this file.

Published file: furkan-crt-computer.glb
Format: glTF 2.0 binary; geometry and all three textures are embedded.
The same self-contained model is used on desktop and mobile.
Integrity and dimensions: model-manifest.json
Verification: node scripts/verify-computer.mjs

The monitor's moving footage is supplied separately by the application.
Its sources and usage notices are preserved in /model-credits.html and
THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md; this model notice does not license that footage.
